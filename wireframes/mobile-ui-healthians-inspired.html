Mobile UI proposal

Healthians-inspired, calmer VitalScan booking experience

The concept borrows the useful patterns: location-first entry, fast test search, at-home sample collection, slot booking, live order status, and smart reports. The upgrade is a quieter clinical hierarchy with fewer competing promos and stronger next-action clarity.

Keep search, location, and cart visible because diagnostics users arrive with intent.
Use teal as the trust signal, not as decoration across every surface.
Make reports feel readable: score, abnormal parameters, recommendation, then full detail.
Design for one-handed booking with sticky CTAs and 48px minimum tap zones.
