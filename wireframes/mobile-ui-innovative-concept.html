Innovative direction

Move from test marketplace to personal health command center

The better UI should not simply sell more packages. It should understand intent, build the right package, guide the pickup journey, and turn lab reports into a readable health story. This concept keeps Healthians-style convenience but makes VitalScan feel smarter and more premium.

01
Pulse home instead of promo homeThe first screen explains what matters today: score, due checks, active pickup, and family alerts.
02
Intent booking instead of blank searchUsers can search, speak, or choose a health goal such as fatigue, fever, diabetes, or annual checkup.
03
Smart package builderShow coverage by organ system, remove duplicates, explain why each test is included, and keep pricing transparent.
04
Report as a storyLead with changes, risk, and doctor questions before the dense PDF-style report.
